Designed a RISC-V FPGA System-on-Chip for matrix-based image processing.

- Used Wishbone to connect a RISC-V CPU and custom hardware accelerator.
- Implemented and validated the design on a Xilinx Artix-7 Nexys 4 DDR board.
- Used VHDL and Verilog with Xilinx Vivado.
