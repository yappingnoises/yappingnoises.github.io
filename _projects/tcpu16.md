Designed and verified a custom 8-bit general-purpose processor with a 16-bit accumulator for an open-source silicon shuttle through Tiny Tapeout.

- Implemented RTL and assertion-based testbenches.
- Verified behavior at RTL and gate level.
- Completed RTL-to-GDSII hardening with LibreLane and the GF180MCU PDK.
